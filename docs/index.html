Design Computed

Architecture × computational design. One short idea a day about how buildings are found, computed and built: form-finding, biomimicry, parametric design and AI for architects.

About

Design Computed is an independent education project by Hala Lahlou, an architect working in computational design. Each short video explains one idea in under 30 seconds with original animated diagrams, real buildings and full credits for every photo and source.

Topics

  • Form-finding: Gaudí's hanging models, Frei Otto's soap films
  • Biomimicry: termite-mound cooling, bone-like structures, algae façades
  • Parametric design: Zaha Hadid, the Louvre Abu Dhabi dome, twisting towers
  • AI for architects: tools, generative design, digital twins
