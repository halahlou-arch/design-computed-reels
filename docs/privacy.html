Privacy policy

This policy covers Design Computed Publisher, the tool the Design Computed team uses to publish its own videos.

What the tool accesses

Only the Design Computed accounts on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, with the permissions needed to upload and publish our own videos and to read basic information about those accounts (name, channel, post links). It does not access anyone else's account or data.

What we store

Access tokens are stored only on the team's own computer to keep the connection active. We keep a local log of what was published and when. Nothing else is collected.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share or transfer any data. We do not use data for advertising. We do not collect data from viewers or other users.

YouTube API Services

Design Computed Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

YouTube data we access

Only: the upload permission (youtube.upload) to publish our videos, and read-only basic information about our own channel (youtube.readonly: channel name and ID). We do not access, collect or store any other YouTube data, and nothing is shared with third parties or shown to anyone.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on our own computer. No YouTube API data is stored beyond the upload confirmation (video ID) in a local log.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the tool stops immediately; the local tokens and log are deleted on request (contact below) within 7 days.
