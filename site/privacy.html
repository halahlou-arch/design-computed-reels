Privacy policy

This policy covers Design Computed Publisher, the tool the Design Computed team uses to publish its own videos.

What the tool accesses

Only the Design Computed accounts on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, with the permissions needed to upload and publish our own videos and to read basic information about those accounts (name, channel, post links). It does not access anyone else's account or data.

What we store

Access tokens are stored only on the team's own computer to keep the connection active. We keep a local log of what was published and when. Nothing else is collected.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share or transfer any data. We do not use data for advertising. We do not collect data from viewers or other users.

Google / YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deletion

Revoking access in the platform's settings stops the tool immediately; local tokens and logs can be deleted on request.
